Bare Metal

The interview trainer that enforces what Copilot took away. No autocomplete. No highlighting. No execution. Just you, a timer, and an interviewer who interrupts.

Stored only in this browser's localStorage. Calls go directly from your browser to api.anthropic.com — no middleman server.
Start in Coach to drill a topic. When you're ready, one button switches you into a timed, graded Interview on a fresh problem — same topic, no warm-up.
House rules
  • The editor is a plain <textarea> — no bracket matching, no syntax color, on purpose. Don't ask for niceties; you won't get them.
  • Talk out loud. Type your thinking into the narration box (or use the mic). Silence over ~35 seconds gets you interrupted, like a real interviewer would.
  • The interviewer watches your code as you type and probes on specific patterns — nested loops, sketchy range() bounds, missing base cases — mid-thought, not at the end.
  • You have 3 hints. Each one is noted in your evaluation.
  • Grading is at a Staff-level bar: classification speed, correctness without execution, and narration quality all count.
